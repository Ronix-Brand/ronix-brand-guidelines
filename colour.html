Colour

The palette is deliberately narrow: a neutral grey ramp carries the interface, and a single brand red carries the signal. Every colour in the system is a variable in the Color collection, named by role rather than by hue.

Global background

Five background steps. Steps 1–4 are a tight light ramp: step 1 is the page, step 2 a resting surface, step 4 a placeholder or disabled fill — only 26 points of lightness separate them.

Step 5 is not part of that ramp. At #0a0a0a it is near-black, used for dark surfaces such as the social card’s Follow button. Reading the set as a smooth progression will mislead you.

Each step has a Rest and a Hover variant. Use the hover variant only on surfaces that are themselves interactive.

  • Global/background/1/Rest #ffffff
  • Global/background/2/Rest #f5f5f5
  • Global/background/3/Rest #f0f0f0
  • Global/background/4/Rest #e5e5e5
  • Global/background/5/Rest #0a0a0a

Global foreground

Foreground is not black. Foreground/1 is #2e2e2e, which keeps long-form text comfortable on white without losing authority.

Foreground/On brand is the only foreground permitted on brand red or on foreground 1.

  • Global/Foreground/1/Rest #2e2e2e
  • Global/Foreground/2/Generic/Rest #424242
  • Global/Foreground/3/Rest #616161
  • Global/Foreground/On brand/Rest #ffffff

Brand

Brand/background/1/Rest is #a30000. It marks the primary action, the active navigation indicator and nothing else by default.

Because it is a signal, its density matters more than its placement. One red element per view is the working rule.

  • Brand/background/1/Rest #a30000
  • Brand/background/1/Hover #8a0000
  • Brand/background/1/Pressed #700000
  • Global/Stroke/1/Global/Rest #dbdbdb

Contrast and pairing

The permitted pairings below all meet WCAG AA for body text. Brand red on white reaches 8.6:1; white on brand red reaches the same, which is why both directions are allowed.

Foreground 3 at #616161 on white reaches 6.0:1, so it clears AA for body text as well as for captions and metadata. It is a de-emphasis colour by intent, not by necessity.
